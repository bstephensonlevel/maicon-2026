LIS Tool Highlight Level.Signal

Stop guessing who's ready to buy. Let the data tell you.

Most marketing systems are great at reporting what happened — but terrible at predicting what to do next. Level.Signal changes that. It's a predictive modeling platform that integrates website behavioral data, lead form submissions, and media interactions to build propensity scores that identify your highest-intent prospects before you spend media dollars chasing them.

Rather than relying on static rules or limited CRM fields, Level.Signal recognizes that buying intent often shows up in behavioral patterns first — visit frequency, content engagement, return behavior — long before someone fills out a form. The model learns which signal combinations correlate with actual downstream revenue, then feeds those quality signals back into your ad platforms to find more people like your best converters.

Think with Google featured Level.Signal as an example of how modern marketers are turning fragmented measurement into predictive growth — moving teams from backward-looking dashboards to forward-looking decisions.
